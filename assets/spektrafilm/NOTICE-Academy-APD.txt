Historical Academy APD/ADX reference

Antitone's Supra paper-only adapter uses derived functional coefficients from
S-2008-002, Academy Density Exchange Encoding (ADX) and the Spectral
Responsivities Defining Academy Printing Density (APD), Version 1.1,
November 18, 2010. This implementation relies on the source's IP
non-enforcement covenant below. It does not claim Academy endorsement or
conformance to a later SMPTE revision.

Source:
https://github.com/aces-aswf/aces-core/blob/v1.0.3/documents/LaTeX/TB-2014-005/S-2008-002.pdf
