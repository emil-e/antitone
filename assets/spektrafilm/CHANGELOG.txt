Antitone modifications:
- Evaluate the deterministic nonspatial pipeline at build time.
- Retain the source camera-negative and printing-media names.
- Factor characteristic curves, DIR/pre-correction, spectral sensitivities,
  dye/base spectra, input-gamut coordinates and enlarger calibration.
- Share the Hanatos input spectral family rather than installing a full
  camera-negative exposure LUT for every stock.
- Calibrate print time on a controlled neutral AP1 0.18 to viewed XYZ Y=0.1
  through the common authoring-reference finish.
  This is a rendering convention, not a photographic or stock-fidelity fit.
- Sample each paper-density readout together with the pinned inverse
  authoring DRT, storing ACEScct/AP1 values.
- Form the paper-only reference through calibrated exposure and effective-dye
  matrices around the shared forward characteristic. Fit standardized colours,
  not photographs, matching common-luminance hue directions and group-mean
  colour strength with equal weighting of Portra Endura, Crystal Archive Type II
  and Ektacolor Edge. Preserve the signed,
  peak-normalized 250D dye basis and base spectrum; add fixed neutral filtration
  with reciprocal lamp scaling to define a passive hypothetical filter over the
  authored input cube. Fixed per-medium printing exposure/balance anchors gray.
  This is a rendering reference, not recovered negative-film chemistry.
- Use common p3rec50 authoring-reference gamut compression without a
  separate lightness rolloff for every medium, in calibration and final readout.
- Bake new treatments on the shared 65-cubed float32 authoring grid.
- Generate compact source conformance observations for native tests.

No source photograph, publisher artwork, Python runtime, grain/halation
profile, camera calibration or measured physical scanner profile is included.
Source hashes, generator identities and numeric component hashes are in
provenance.json. LICENSE-data.txt and LICENSE-code.txt are retained unchanged.
