## Contract

- Apple Silicon/macOS first; resource policy follows observed needs, with no assumed minimum chip/RAM baseline. Proposed deployment minimum is macOS 14+, subject to pinned API/dependency requirements.
- LibRaw development reused without a custom demosaicer or camera hacks; float camera-color conversion into the initial AP0 inversion basis; roll-fixed development WB. Convert after inversion to AP1/ACEScg for photographic processing, independently of the inversion basis.
- Interactive GPU inversion/edits and OCIO-generated ACES 2 rendering (option B). OCIO CPU processing is the export/reference path, not the interactive-viewer fallback.
- Four pickers: Dmin (film base), Dmax, roll-gamma neutral, photo-WB neutral. Full-resolution adjustable patches/rectangles; one patch per correction; unusable samples explain and make no changes.
- Gamma and WB wheels with sliders/numerical controls over the same state. Gamma picking preserves global gamma/exposure and ignores photo WB. Photo picking changes only that photo's WB and moves the displayed controls to the solved values.
- No positive preview/export until both valid references exist. An explicit developed-source negative view remains available and does not change export processing.
- Display P3/D65 (default wide option) and sRGB 16-bit rendered TIFF, with matching 100-nit SDR rendering/limiting and encoding gamut/ICC. Both use the sRGB segmented transfer. ProPhoto and SDR Rec.2020 were rejected after color-collapse gates; the P3 replacement was explicitly approved, not hidden in a wider container. Preview follows the selected rendering, then managed monitor conversion.
- Scene-linear AP0 EXR, converted from AP1 working RGB at export, half/full float; reject non-finite/half-overflow rather than silently clipping. EXR excludes output rendering and monitor conversion.
- Saved calibration is independent of filmstrip membership. Removing a calibration source does not invalidate its measured value. Proposed changed-content behavior: flag fingerprint mismatches, require explicit source acceptance, and never silently resample.
- No flat field, crop/rotation, contrast/pivot, vectorscope, new inversion spaces, or later-platform implementation. Do not alter sample RAWs or commit them. No commits/pushes are authorized by this plan.
