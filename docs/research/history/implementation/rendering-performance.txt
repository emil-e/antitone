## Goal and agreed scope

Prioritize responsiveness for typical rolls of up to about 36 photos while scaling optional caches, prefetch and concurrency down under pressure. Preserve exact RGB16 source/development identity, pinned LibRaw/ACES, numerical tolerances, source-coordinate correction, cancellation and selected-image priority. No whole-roll developed RAW cache, alternate development, approximate ACES, schema change or CPU interactive fallback.

Approved work covered rectangular overlap dispatch, immutable renderer sharing/prewarm, selected-Scan thumbnail seeding, protected visible/recent caches, disposable persistent proxies, phase measurements and bounded submission-depth experiments. The user subsequently approved pinned checkout-local OpenMP, bounded CPU concurrency, lazy diagnostics and semantic integration with committed geometry/scopes. Higher-resolution switching caches and import fingerprinting were assessed, not implemented.
