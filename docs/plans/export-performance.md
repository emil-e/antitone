Reduce complete work producing final full-resolution pixels from an already-developed authoritative source: required source/repair preparation, photographic processing, reconstruction and readback through ready-to-encode pixels. Measure codec/compression, destination I/O, cold source acquisition and batch throughput separately. Improve complete latency rather than isolated calls whose gain is hidden by overlap.
