# Local photographic adjustment acceptance

Source-owned Global/local controls, ordered list actions, linear/radial transactions and normal device-aware Open/Save/source/correction/Analyze/export admission are implemented. The [editor](../editor.md#immutable-local-grade-routing), [processing](../processing.md#local-grades-change-controls-not-rendered-pixels), [geometry](../geometry.md#source-anchored-local-masks) and [project](../projects.md#source-owned-local-adjustments) guides own current behavior. This plan tracks unfinished acceptance and usability, not another tool implementation. [PLAN.md](../../PLAN.md) owns priorities.

## Guarantees to preserve

Keep model-4 native-camera inversion, signed/HDR AP1, original-source coordinates, immutable accepted snapshots and all numerical/range/precision gates. Global Roll/Photo processing precedes ordered locals; composition stays whole-photo. Inspection and calibration/global fitting bypass locals. Original GPU export/reference evaluates original pixel centres before target/AP1 reconstruction; the working view grades filtered neutral AP1 and evaluates masks at mapped footprint centres. These are deliberately different sampling algorithms, not competing export authorities.

Complete candidates validate before mutation, including retained offscreen/non-filmstrip grades and inactive records. Unsupported edits keep accepted data/resources; rejected creation remains retryable, and unaccepted creation consumes no accepted ID. Analyze explicitly warns before resetting ordered Photo grades and removing all their local records/masks, retaining allocator history and nonordered records. Section resets retain masks. No CPU fallback, source clamp, arbitrary stage cap or weaker validator may be introduced to make a demonstration pass.

Conservative support depends on calibration, response, Global grade and the ordered local stack. Mild mixed stacks can reach the existing normal-precision boundary; this remains a usability limit, not a fixed count cap or proven arithmetic failure. Any support improvement needs evidence at its actual mathematical/representation boundary, not a relaxed tolerance.

No brushes, raster/AI/compound masks, local inversion/calibration/neutral fitting, cross-photo copying, automatic suggestions, recovery or general undo are implied. New ovals provisionally use a broad soft fade, full strength and identity controls; hard edges and valid small ovals remain available. Those creation defaults do not redefine the persisted smoothstep/weighted-control contract.

## Remaining acceptance

### Photographic correspondence and feel

Exercise representative skin, noisy shadows, coloured light, fine texture and broad/fractional exposure, WB, contrast/Colour preservation and saturation/Density changes. Include overlap/order, off-image geometry, soft/hard boundaries, small ellipses, corrected inputs, crop/orientation/straightening and both targets. Inspect Fit/native and reopened TIFF/AP0 EXR: export should read as the same photograph with refined detail, not a surprising tone/colour/local-effect change.

Quantitative charts come before another gallery. Existing original/scalar references distinguish arithmetic error from filtering-before-grading and footprint-centre-mask approximation. High-frequency/signed-HDR adversaries can show substantial approximation differences even when implementations agree. Small RAW patches, identical-input shader agreement and encoding metadata do not establish photographic acceptance. Investigate observed failures before proposing a more elaborate representation or another rendering owner.

Judge draw → grade → refine with release acceptance, direct handles, effective-direction/Invert cues, optional coverage and normal/narrow panel layouts in those photographs. Confirm that per-gesture Escape and rejection preserve earlier grading without implying general undo. Judge the provisional radial fade and handle behavior in that workflow. Do not add falloff families, wider strength, snapping or a radius floor without a demonstrated need and an explicit interaction decision.

### Integrated interaction and ownership

The single normal working-view lifecycle must retain coherent detail/grade/scopes during edits, navigation, source return and target changes. Check rapid key churn, blocked/stale work, popup/fullscreen precedence and explicitly requested original comparison; no unrequested idle original refinement, duplicate provisional scopes or recursive preview resampling.

Complete input/queue/presentation observations under representative interactions and account for current/building/completed/encoded/in-flight/native-retired resources alongside ordinary source leases. The [bounded local diagnostic](../diagnostics.md#local-processing-and-reuse) covers authored or supplied native-size input, correction, two locals, edits/target reuse and an encoded-copy consumer. It excludes scopes/native host; process footprint/resident and Metal allocation overlap. Passing it is not general pressure, native-shell or broader-device acceptance. Preserve existing safety stops and label uncoordinated service timings indicative.

Physical pointer/trackpad, monitor interpretation, unoccluded native presentation and broader-device checks remain separate from headless tests. Native application automation, setup or consequential pressure experiments require authorization. Do not turn this broad unfinished acceptance into an indefinite prerequisite for maintaining the supported tools.
