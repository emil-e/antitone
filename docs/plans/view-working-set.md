## Acceptance contract

Export should elicit **“Yes, that is what I saw”**: the same exposure, colour, tone and local effects, potentially with better-resolved detail, grain and boundaries. Numerical preview/export noncommutativity alone is not photographic rejection, and software agreement is not photographic acceptance.

At a fixed view, controls must update a stable-detail image throughout dragging and idle. Neither blur-during-drag/sharpen-after-release nor a sharp but unresponsive stale grade is acceptable. Navigation may transform retained support while replacement acquisition returns to originals. Work follows physical display pixels, including scale; 1080p/4K are workloads, not caps or promises.

Preserve native-camera inversion, continuous correction, signed/HDR values, original-coordinate masks, whole-candidate validation and existing numerical gates. Original-sample reconstruction remains reference/export authority. Inspection, fitting and full-resolution picks keep their bypasses. No universal low-detail replacement, recursive preview resampling, arbitrary stage cap or CPU interactive renderer. Coarse coverage may fill missing support only; its retention and composition cost must remain explicit.

Initial live Global appearance and responsiveness have been positively assessed. Prioritize quantitative and synthetic stress verification next, rather than another static photographic gallery; that feedback does not establish matching-view export, aggregate resources or broader device/physical acceptance.

## Remaining work

- Broaden production stable-f32 arithmetic/acquisition evidence beyond the exercised authored cases and Sony photograph, across representative cameras/ordered stacks and devices. Distinguish factorization/storage rounding, filtering-before-grading and footprint-centre masks. [Diagnostics](../diagnostics.md#stable-float32-view-grading) owns reproduction and limitations.
- Exercise source/photo/document retirement, correction/calibration changes, target changes, physical scale, native permutations, straightening, partial coverage and rapid navigation. Check latest-control freshness, bounded cancellation and viewer/scope agreement, not merely cache keys.
- Measure complete acquisition/edit/scopes/presentation latency and residual ordered-local cost with representative corrected/uncorrected 1080p/4K photographs. Do not substitute isolated-shader timings or UI readback cost for interactive completion.
- Establish peak concurrent ownership/admission for mandatory current/building/completed support, temporary originals, rendered replacements, optional references and encoded/in-flight/native-retired versions. Zero optional retention and pressure must preserve mandatory selected detail without recycling waits or hidden allocations. Logical entry counts, declared buffer bytes and Arc tests are not aggregate native-memory evidence; RSS and native allocation measurements overlap and must not be added.
- Quantify matching-view preview/reference/export correspondence with ramps, fine texture, grain-like noise, shadow colour, strong Global edits and soft/hard boundaries. Separate arithmetic, sampling and stale-grade/coverage defects. Preserve the positive live appearance/feel evidence without treating it as all-case acceptance. Normal idle must neither build an unrequested original reference nor replace working detail; explicit reference completion must remain a same-position comparison.
- Broaden device/camera evidence and complete unoccluded physical input/presentation checks. Offscreen GPU completion establishes neither scanout nor physical responsiveness.
