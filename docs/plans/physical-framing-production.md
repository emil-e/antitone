# Shared spatial metadata acceptance

The [shared preparation](../spatial-metadata.md) is connected to document lifetime, project persistence, Analyze and Auto-crop. Either consumer can request reusable source/group regions without accepting the other's photographic state. Headless authored-source checks cover both consumer orders, real acquisition, cancellation/physical cleanup, source freshness, captured writes and crop Apply/Cancel/undo. These checks do not establish photographic framing or physical interaction acceptance.

Common W/H represent fitted measurement variation; complete-frame peers and a special reference-photo workflow are not prerequisites. Measured, inferred, camera-limited and unknown components remain distinct. Sampling insets and photographic margins are downstream policies, not replacements for underlying boundaries.

## Remaining acceptance

- **Current photographs and development.** Check framing/content preservation on current corrected Sony, HDR and direct-DNG sources, including real partial/clipped frames and photographic neighbours. Consumed older camera-code replays and supplied native hypotheses do not establish current-development, blind discovery or independent whole-picture accuracy. Retain annotation conflicts and small wanted-fringe diagnostics rather than tuning to erase them.
- **Composed physical interaction.** Exercise Photo/Roll/selection entry, deferred preparation feedback/cancellation, review browsing, crop margins, Apply/Cancel and source/Save/Open transitions in normal use. Headless controllers and offscreen painting do not establish native keyboard/pointer or photographic feel.
- **Resources and latency.** Assess normal-worker preparation across complete rolls. The shared coarse pass and Analyze's existing sampling/fine pass are separate; bounded captures do not establish acceptable aggregate memory, wall time or pressure behaviour. Keep physical admission/leases through native cleanup and do not introduce a correctness memory guard or another RAW pool.
- **Broader dependency/failure cases.** Extend coverage when actual source/redevelopment/correction/code-basis failures expose a missing boundary. Shared measurement excludes the optional roll flat-field, so changing that correction alone is not a reason to redetect. Embedded camera correction remains part of source interpretation. Refreshes must never silently change accepted crops, sampling or grades.
- **Remaining consumers.** Physical bare-light/reference support and future source-region consumers remain separate from statistical sampling support and this explicit Auto-crop adapter; [region detection](region-detection.md) owns that scope.

## Detector limits

Native-family and placement searches are bounded, not exhaustive ambiguity proofs. Camera-limit inference uses bounded continuation evidence with no calibrated photographic error rate. Common size is a useful chosen estimate, not proof of literal physical equality. All-clipped authored cases cover complementary measured axes and useful visible framing with unknown full height; broader real clipped rolls still need photographic evidence.

Keep the useful native joint-plus-two-adjacent-edge baseline unless a practical observed defect warrants a focused change. This acceptance work does not imply another precision or algorithm campaign.
