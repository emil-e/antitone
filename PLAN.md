# Antitone — roadmap

## Current state

The editor supports native-camera model-4 inversion, roll flat-field correction, Analyze roll, full-resolution calibration, crop/straightening, Photo contrast/pivot and saturation, GPU previews and visible-image scopes that follow pan/zoom, thumbnails, Save/Open and TIFF/AP0 EXR export. [Topic guides](docs/README.md#understand-the-system) explain the models and important boundaries; [architecture](docs/architecture.md) maps the implementation.

[Local gradient adjustments](docs/plans/local-adjustments.md) have scalar/persistence/export and GPU snapshot foundations, including selected/pending preview, thumbnail, scope and refinement routing. Normal editor admission and local tools are paused behind the shared [view-working-set package](docs/plans/view-working-set.md). The Global editor prototype now caches a filtered, physical-view-sized linear source across grading changes and reacquires from originals after navigation. It coalesces requests on the existing backend, fills missing navigation support from an independently acquired coarse whole-composition AP1 underlay, shares one immutable detail/underlay target-linear result with scopes and offers an explicitly requested same-position original-resolution GPU reference. Normal idle neither builds hidden original results nor replaces working detail; original tools/comparison use a purpose-bound service, while provisional proxies retain one source and lazy active-target output. Ordinary source inspection now shares the physical-detail/underlay lifecycle with its own pre-inversion source identity, including before calibration; inspection brightness reuses that source. Export should refine the preview's detail, not reinterpret its grade. Supported-domain usability, complete latency and aggregate/in-flight memory acceptance remain unfinished; existing numerical gates and exact settled/export reconstruction are preserved.

The provisional manual Dust workflow uses direct canvas selection and release-to-commit edits. Its unchanged healer evaluates ungraded AP1 after correction/inversion/camera conversion, before photographic Roll/Photo grades. Sparse GPU repair snapshots feed the retained working detail/underlay and original reference; scopes follow the same accepted image. Repairs remain unsaved/unexported and thumbnails remain unrepaired. [Photographic, latency/resource and physical-input acceptance](docs/plans/dust-repair.md) is still open; persistence, automatic proposals and output consumers are deferred.

The workspace uses Roll → Photo → Rendering stages, collapsible inspector sections, independently saved UI preferences and stable viewer-bar tool identity/Apply/Cancel. The visual grouping is accepted; physical input, preference relaunch and broader photographic/device acceptance remain open. [Interaction follow-up](docs/plans/inspection-and-interaction.md). Shared presentation theme/typography/metrics/actions, viewer tool groups, inspector sections/tabs/labelled rows, contact-sheet cards/captions, blocking decision dialogs, acknowledged flat-field/save/export failures and agent reuse rules are implemented. Working feedback uses one typed foreground task lifecycle with concise modal/inline/activity-strip presentation, completed-file Import/Open progress and status-bar-only export, with Analyze progress/Cancel available across stages and optional background feedback kept local. [Broader failure coverage, additional native/export progress and cancellation, and native acceptance](docs/plans/completion.md#operation-feedback) remain unfinished.

Memory-256 cached pending selected previews and visible-thumbnail admission/repaint are implemented and accepted for current use. Filmstrip-only embedded camera-preview placeholders provide navigation while proper thumbnails develop, preserving card layout and edge clipping. [Broader interaction/resource and photographic acceptance](docs/plans/visual-feedback.md) remain open. No selected-view JPEG adoption or persistent JPEG cache is selected.

Immutable full-resolution source reuse, persistent CPU/GPU optional byte limits, eager proper CPU/disk thumbnails and one native priority worker are implemented. Lazy support hints and navigation during pending photo loads pass authored/controller/composed tests and a bounded retained-hit exercise. Broader normal-budget residency, pressure/aggregate-memory and hardware acceptance remain [resource follow-up](docs/plans/visual-feedback.md); the dedicated Preferences dialog is deferred.

Photo-only full-display inspection uses current-Space borderless window coverage, zoom-group entry and auto-hiding shared controls. Controller/composed checks and the bounded native display-frame/active-Space/paint/window-restoration gate pass. [Broader native lifecycle, physical input and display acceptance](docs/plans/inspection-and-interaction.md#layout-and-controls) remain open.

Roll multi-selection and confirmed project-only removal preserve RAWs, retained source grades and Roll calibration/correction. The active photo has distinct card framing, and empty rolls can be saved/reopened and repopulated. [Physical-input and accessibility acceptance](docs/plans/inspection-and-interaction.md#roll-workflow) remains open; undo is separate.

Analyze's [initializer and sampling-area review](docs/roll-analysis.md) are accepted for current use. Compatible captures strengthen a conservative shared holder interior, with independent picture evidence and per-photo exceptions reviewed in the retained inspector. Support and grading install atomically under unchanged numerical checks. [Photographic acceptance and deferred sampling/dense-tail work](docs/plans/roll-assistance.md) remain follow-ups; [bare-light correction support and future crop consumers](docs/plans/region-detection.md) require separate scope. The bounded detector is not an automatic crop or proof of material accuracy. Recovery/relink/undo remains lower priority than interaction and photographic acceptance.

## Remaining work, in priority order

1. **Remaining region consumers:** scope physical bare-light/reference support and future picture/crop acceptance separately from the delivered Analyze review. Do not treat statistical support as a physical boundary or install correction/crops automatically. [Scope](docs/plans/region-detection.md).
2. **Responsiveness and resources:** real-use scrolling/control feedback, actual memory-pressure exercise and aggregate memory assessment. Measure switching caches or source-loading costs only for a concrete need. [Scope](docs/plans/completion.md#rendering-and-thumbnail-performance).
3. **Photographic acceptance:** camera inversion, native/settled straightening agreement, rotated neutral picks, corrected/uncorrected Save/Open and TIFF/AP0 EXR reopen; full-resolution evaluation of the implemented [contrast/saturation tools](docs/processing.md#photo-contrast-and-saturation). [Geometry](docs/plans/completion.md#geometry), [photographic controls](docs/plans/completion.md#photographic-controls-and-bw-handling).
4. **UI polish, scopes and interaction:** scope-guide/composed-photo appearance, wheel accessibility, physical trackpad/preference-relaunch exercise, decision-dialog acceptance/native-shell design and dark-red/cold-shadow diagnosis. [Scopes](docs/plans/completion.md#scopes), [interaction](docs/plans/inspection-and-interaction.md), [thumbnails](docs/plans/visual-feedback.md).
5. **Completion features:** additional photographic controls/B&W design, shared operation feedback and source readability. [Scope](docs/plans/completion.md).
6. **Export and release:** batch/progress/cancellation/metadata, dependency bundling/licensing, clean-machine testing, macOS 14, broader cameras/devices and physical-monitor matching/transitions. [Scope](docs/plans/completion.md#export-and-release-work).
7. **Persistence:** missing/changed-source UI, recovery and undo/redo. [Scope](docs/plans/durable-projects.md).

## Limits

Numerical and headless checks do not establish photographic, physical-input or monitor acceptance. No minimum chip/RAM target or universal numerical-domain coverage is established. Native runtime paths are checkout-local, not distributable packaging. Lightroom/Resolve interoperability remains unverified. Windows/HDR display output and custom RAW development are outside current scope.

Setup: [README](README.md). Checks: [development](docs/development.md), [diagnostics](docs/diagnostics.md).
